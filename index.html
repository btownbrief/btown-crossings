HOW TO PLAY

Build one word per turn in a straight line, crossing what's already down. The first word must cover City Hall Park ⭐ in the middle. Every run of letters it creates must be a real word — the board checks as you go.

Can't play? SWAP tiles (that's your turn) or PASS. Use all 7 tiles in one turn for the Full Bucket: +40.

North Beach — letter ×2
Waterfront — letter ×3
Church Street — word ×2
the Intervale — word ×3

Premium squares pay out only on the turn they're first covered. Game ends when the bag is empty and someone plays their last tile (they collect everyone's leftover tile points) — or after two full rounds of passes. High score wins.
